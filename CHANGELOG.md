# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- a picker wheel over the people on the board, the stickies you select or a list you type
- six modes: Wheel, Order, Groups, Dice, Coin and Warm-up
- everyone on the board sees the same spin land on the same answer at the same moment, and the result stays as a sticky
- groups by number or by size, never more than one person apart
- one to three dice with 6 or 20 sides
- 120 warm-up questions, 60 in English and 60 in Thai, skipping the ones you drew lately
- a wheel over stickies selects the winning sticky and takes you to it
- Remove winner takes the last winner out of the list or the stickies before the next spin
- an optional tick sound that only you hear
- the panel follows the board's light and dark themes
