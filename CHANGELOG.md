# Changelog

## 2.0.0

The wheel lives on the board

- a wheel is part of the board, so everyone on it watches the same spin land on the same pick at the same moment, even people without Spin; anyone who opens the board later sees where it landed
- press **Spin** in the middle of the wheel or in the panel; the pick comes from crypto randomness on the screen of whoever pressed it
- a landing gets confetti, a lime spotlight on the winning slice and a **Picked** ribbon under the wheel that stays
- edit the selected wheel in the panel: type a name and press Enter, paste a list, rename, delete, and give each entry an emoji or a photo, up to 20 entries
- **Remove picked names** takes each winner off the wheel until you press **Reset**, on the wheel or in the panel
- start from **People here**, **Yes or no**, **Lunch ideas**, or warm-up questions in English or Thai
- **New wheel** and **Duplicate**, for as many wheels on one board as you like
- each viewer sees the wheel in their own light or dark theme
- removed: dice, coin, order, groups and the warm-up card mode, to focus on the wheel; the warm-up questions live on as wheel presets, and result cards from 1.0.0 stay on boards as plain stickies

## 1.0.0

First public release

- a picker wheel over the people on the board, the stickies you select or a list you type
- six modes: Wheel, Order, Groups, Dice, Coin and Warm-up
- everyone on the board sees the same spin land on the same answer at the same moment, and the result stays as a sticky
- groups by number or by size, never more than one person apart
- one to three dice with 6 or 20 sides
- 120 warm-up questions, 60 in English and 60 in Thai, skipping the ones you drew lately
- a wheel over stickies selects the winning sticky and takes you to it
- Remove winner takes the last winner out of the list or the stickies before the next spin
- an optional tick sound that only you hear
- the panel follows the board's light and dark themes
